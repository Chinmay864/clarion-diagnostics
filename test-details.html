Diagnostic Test Details

Urinalysis Routine Screening

Test Name:
Routine Urinalysis Panel
Purpose:
To analyze urine composition and detect physical, chemical, and microscopic indicators of metabolic or renal health.
Sample Required:
Mid-stream urine specimen in a sterile container.
Preparation Instructions:
Collect the sample in a clean container following standard hygiene guidelines.
Normal Reference Range:
Standard reference ranges vary based on individual physiological parameters and clinical testing methods.
Report Delivery Time:
Same-day digital delivery for routine processing.

Important Patient Instructions

Fasting Requirements: Fasting may be required depending on accompanying test panels.
Please strictly follow all collection instructions provided by the lab.
Reference intervals may vary across different testing technologies and laboratory equipment.
