Test Information & Glossary

CBC
Complete Blood Count evaluates overall health and measures key cellular components.
HbA1c
Glycated Hemoglobin measures average blood glucose levels over the preceding 2 to 3 months.
LFT
Liver Function Test assesses liver enzymes, proteins, and bilirubin levels.
KFT
Kidney Function Test evaluates renal filtration efficiency and fluid balance.
ECG
Electrocardiogram records the electrical activity and rhythm of the heart.
